# Dybbo brand

![Dybbo logo](../web/public/brand/dybbo-logo.svg)

## The idea

Dybbo is named after the *dybbuk*, a spirit that takes over a body for a while and leaves when it is driven out. The mark is that idea in one shape: a **D** (the host) whose counter is a **one-eyed wisp** (the spirit inside it). The wisp's head is round and its body trails back toward the D's spine, where it came in. The eye glances up and forward: curious and a little mischievous, like Chog.

It is one flat shape with the wisp cut out of it, so it works in a single colour, needs no gradient, and keeps the read of a "D" down to a 16px favicon.

| Slime green | `#3ddc97` |

Use the deep-green mark on light grounds and the slime mark on deep green. Use black or white when only one colour is available.

## Rules

- Keep clear space around the mark of at least the wisp's width on every side.
- Don't put the eye back on the mark below 24px; use the favicon file.
- Don't outline, rotate, stretch, recolour the wisp separately, or add effects.
- The wordmark is Chillax Semibold (Fontshare), converted to outlines. In the lockup, the mark runs 1.2× the cap height and is centred on the caps.
